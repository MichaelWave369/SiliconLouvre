# Visitor Guide — The Stillness That Moves

**Welcome to The Silicon Louvre.** Exhibition 001 is a collection of six original static optical-art studies. Nothing on the artwork canvases is animated.

## How to visit

1. From the homepage, find **The Stillness That Moves** and choose **Begin the Gallery Walk**. Your tour begins at stop 01.
2. Examine each framed image at your own pace. The wall plaque explains the visual prompt, technique and credit.
3. Use **Next Work** or **Previous Work** to move through all six works. The numbered buttons jump directly to a stop. The tour loops after stop 06.
4. Select **Artwork Only** to hide the wall plaque and enlarge the presentation area. **Show Plaque** brings it back.
5. Select **Read Details** for the expanded scientific/curatorial description. You can enter gallery view again from any artwork card.
6. Choose **Share** to copy a link directly to the selected artwork. **Save** remembers your favorites in your browser only; there is no account or cloud synchronization.

## Keyboard controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Left arrow | Previous artwork |
| Right arrow | Next artwork |
| G | Switch between gallery and detail views |
| F | Show or hide the center focus point |
| Esc | Exit the artwork dialog |
| Tab / Shift+Tab | Move through dialog controls |

Every keyboard action also has a visible button equivalent. On mobile, the tour includes touch-friendly numbered stops and navigation controls.

## Viewing with care

- The exhibits are static. **Perceived motion does not measure stress, anxiety or neurological health.**
- Some visitors will perceive movement; others will see little or none. Both are normal.
- Try looking away from the center, shifting gaze, or using the **focus point** to compare experiences.
- If the high-contrast designs feel uncomfortable, exit the dialog or stop viewing. There is no requirement to complete the tour.
- Nothing autoplays, flashes, or emits audio. Animation is not necessary for these works.
- Respect browser zoom and your operating system's reduced-motion preferences.

## Artwork credits and provenance

All six gallery studies in v0.1–v0.2 are original source-rendered SVG illustrations made for The Silicon Louvre with human-directed artistic decisions and AI coding assistance. They are **not the previously generated image files** from the gallery concept session. Those could be curated into a future exhibition with independent credit and asset provenance.

The Silicon Louvre is an independent, free-to-view project, not affiliated with the Musée du Louvre in Paris. Human review will govern future submissions, public artist credits, and permissions.

## For exhibit contributors (future)

A future artist-submission workflow should require:
- the actual submitted artwork (rather than a hotlink);
- artist identities or approved pseudonyms, clearly distinguishing human direction and AI involvement;
- tool/model provenance when known;
- permission to publish and a declared artwork license;
- curator review and the ability to withdraw work under appropriate terms;
- accessibility descriptions and safety review for flashing/moving exhibits.

The current site does not accept submissions or run any agent publishing automation.
